Design notes
• Button label changes from "Link Student" → "Send Invite" once email is verified
• Button is disabled (dimmed) while email is not verified — prevents sending blind invites
• Pending / Accepted / Declined states are all visible in coach's notification tab
• Student's notification shows Accept + Decline inline — no extra screen needed
• Once declined, coach can re-invite after 7 days (prevent spam)
